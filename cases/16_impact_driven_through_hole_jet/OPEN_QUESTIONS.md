# Open experimental questions

The current images do not establish the inner lower/upper diameters, lower or
upper inner-edge chamfer/radius, outer-edge radius, roughness, coating, or face
parallelism.  The most valuable next measurement is the **inner lower-edge
radius/chamfer**, followed by separate lower- and upper-mouth diameters.  These
directly control the impact pressure turning into the aperture.

The solver reserves rectangular defaults conceptually as
`inner_radius_lower=inner_radius_upper=5.05 mm` and zero edge radii.  No cone
or fillet is activated without measurement.
